Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of the information umask processes to verify staff credentials, manage elevated sessions and keep server owners informed.

Draft for review. Before publishing, specify the operator and contact details in config.js, decide a concrete data-retention and deletion policy, verify hosting providers and legal grounds for processing, and review this text for accuracy.

1. Information we process

Discord server and account identifiers

We process the server ID, owner ID, relevant staff-member IDs, role IDs, selected session-duration settings and, if configured, an alert-channel ID. To display account names and check staff access, umask may retrieve current Discord member or username information via Discord's API.

Authentication credentials

Each staff member's ten-word authentication phrase is associated with their Discord user ID and server ID. umask stores a salted, one-way Argon2id verifier for future authentication. To allow initial one-time collection, a Fernet-encrypted copy may be stored for up to 15 minutes and is erased when collected or expired. The plain phrase may be transmitted through Discord's interaction systems when issued, collected or entered. It is not intentionally written to application logs.

Session and security records

We store credential issue and collection status, failed authentication attempts or temporary lockouts, active session start and expiry times, and a history of completed sessions and termination reasons. The server owner can view credential metadata and session history; stored phrases and password hashes are not shown in that dashboard.

2. Why we process it

To check whether a staff member is authorised, verify their phrase, grant and remove the selected temporary role, recover expired sessions after restarts, prevent repeated guessing, and provide owner-visible activity records. We do not need ordinary message content for these features.

3. Storage, security and access

In the current self-hosted implementation, operational records are kept in a SQLite database. Authentication phrases are verified through one-way password hashes after initial collection. A temporary encrypted copy is used only for initial delivery. People operating the service and its hosting infrastructure may have administrative access to this data and its backups. Server owners can view relevant credential and session metadata through the bot's private owner dashboard.

Do not assume the service has been independently audited. These controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. How long records are kept

The encrypted one-time delivery copy expires or is removed within 15 minutes in normal operation. A credential-verifier record remains until the credential is revoked, replaced or otherwise deleted. The current prototype does not automatically purge historical session records after a fixed period. Session records and backups may persist until the operator removes them. Before public launch, the operator should adopt and publish an explicit retention schedule, backup retention period, and deletion procedure.

5. Deletion and requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact . The operator may need to verify that the request comes from an authorised server owner or the affected user. Some records may be necessary to investigate abuse or to meet legal obligations. The current bot does not provide an automated complete-account data export or deletion command; requests may require manual operator action.

6. Third parties

The bot communicates with Discord to receive commands, read relevant role and user information, and assign/remove roles. Discord processes interaction data under its own policies. The website is served by Cloudflare Pages if deployed as described in the project guide; Cloudflare may process ordinary web requests and technical connection data under its own policies. No third-party advertising or analytics scripts are included in this website package.

7. Website visitors

This static website does not ask visitors to create accounts, enter passwords or submit personal information. It does not include tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or client-side analytics cookies. Visiting the site still causes standard network requests to the hosting provider, which may collect technical logs under its own policies. Following an install or support link takes you to a third-party service.

8. International use and legal rights

If privacy law applies to the operator or the users of the service, the operator must identify an appropriate legal basis for processing, applicable international transfer arrangements, data-subject rights and response procedures before public release. Contact with privacy questions or requests.

9. Updates

This policy may change when bot features, hosting, retention periods or data practices change. The updated policy should show its effective date, and material changes should be communicated to affected communities as appropriate.
