Elevated permissions for Discord staff. Only when needed.

umask gives staff the elevated role you choose after they authenticate, then removes it when their session ends. You remain in control of the role and its permissions.

How it works

Setup and authentication happen entirely in Discord.

  1. 1

    Choose the roles

    The server owner uses /setup to select the permanent staff role and the elevated role.

  2. 2

    Issue a private code

    The owner runs /issue. Each staff member collects their phrase using /receive and stores it securely.

  3. 3

    Authenticate when needed

    Staff use /authenticate to receive the elevated role for an allowed duration. It is removed on expiry or with /signout.

What umask needs

Just enough access to assign the role you've configured. No changes to its permissions.

Manage Roles
Allows umask to grant and remove your elevated role.
A higher role position
Place the umask bot role directly above the elevated role in Discord's role list.

umask doesn't need Administrator, access to message content, or the permissions contained in the elevated role. NOTE: The elevated role cannot have the Administrator or the Manage Roles permissions.

A safeguard, not a guarantee.

umask reduces the time staff accounts hold elevated permissions. It cannot prevent misuse during an active session or guarantee immediate role removal during an outage. Read the limitations.
