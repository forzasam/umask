01 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
All in Discord.

Staff can use their usual Discord permissions when they're signed in. No external dashboard.

  1. 01

    Owner sets up access

    Choose your staff and elevated roles with /setup, then issue each staff member a private code with /issue.

  2. 02

    Staff collect their code

    Each staff member runs /receive once and keeps their code somewhere safe.

  3. 03

    Sign in when needed

    Staff use /authenticate to receive the elevated role for a set time. It is removed automatically at expiry, or earlier with /signout.

02 / ROLE SETUP

Minimum access.
Clear setup.

You define the elevated role’s permissions. umask only grants and removes that role.

What umask needs

Manage Roles
To grant and remove your chosen elevated role.
Role position
Place the umask bot role directly above the elevated-permissions role. No higher is necessary.

No Administrator permission. No message-content access. umask does not need the permissions contained in the elevated role or edit its permissions.

umask reduces exposure to compromised accounts while staff are signed out. It cannot protect an active elevated session or a stolen access code. Unexpected downtime may delay role removal. Privacy & data handling ↗
