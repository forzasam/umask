01 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, all in Discord.

No external dashboard or account to manage.

01

Choose the role.

The owner uses /setup to select the staff role, elevated role and allowed session lengths.

02

Issue a code.

Each staff member receives a unique, owner-issued phrase through /issue and /receive.

03

Authenticate.

Staff use /authenticate when needed. Their elevated role is removed on sign-out or expiry.

02 / ROLE SETUP

Your permissions.
Our timing.

Set the elevated role's permissions yourself. umask only assigns and removes that role for authorised staff members.

The bot needs Manage Roles and the permissions of the elevated role. Place its role directly above the elevated role in Discord's role hierarchy—no higher than necessary.

It does not require Administrator or access to ordinary message content.

View the role setup guide ↗

03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Clear limits.
No big promises.

umask reduces the time powerful staff permissions are available. It doesn't stop misuse during an active session, and it can't protect a phrase that's been stolen.

If umask goes offline unexpectedly, Discord may retain active elevated roles beyond their intended expiry. The bot checks and reconciles sessions when it reconnects.
