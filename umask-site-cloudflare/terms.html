1. The service

umask is a Discord bot that allows a Discord server owner to nominate a permanent staff role and a separate elevated-permissions role. Authorised staff members may authenticate using an owner-issued secret phrase to receive the elevated role temporarily. The bot attempts to remove the role when the session ends.

2. Who may use it

You must comply with Discord's applicable Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and platform rules. Server owners are responsible for deciding who may receive authentication credentials and how their server's roles and permissions are configured. The bot is intended for lawful, authorised administration of Discord communities.

3. Owner and staff responsibilities

  • Configure the bot's permissions and role position according to the minimum needed for your chosen elevated role.
  • Remove protected permissions from permanent staff roles and review other routes that could bypass umask.
  • Issue credentials only to trusted staff members, and verify their identity before issuance or replacement.
  • Keep authentication phrases private and, preferably, outside Discord. Revoke a phrase if you suspect it has been exposed.
  • Keep the server owner account secure and maintain a manual recovery path in case the bot is unavailable.

4. What umask does not guarantee

umask reduces the time during which elevated permissions are assigned; it does not guarantee protection from all account compromises. In particular, it cannot prevent abuse during an active session, a compromise of the server owner, theft of a staff member's authentication phrase, or alternative permission-granting routes. The bot may be unavailable due to infrastructure or Discord API outages.

Role expiry is not guaranteed during downtime. Discord may retain an elevated role after its recorded expiry if the bot is disconnected or unable to reach the Discord API. The bot attempts to reconcile overdue sessions when it is operational again. An owner should be prepared to remove roles manually when necessary.

5. Changes and availability

The operator may update, suspend or discontinue the service for maintenance, security or operational reasons. Features and supported Discord permissions may change. Material changes to these terms should be communicated appropriately before they take effect.

6. Data and privacy

Use of umask involves processing Discord identifiers, server configuration, credential-verification data and session records. The Privacy Policy explains the current data handling. Do not use the service in circumstances where the described processing is inappropriate for your community.

7. Disclaimer and responsibility

umask is provided as a focused security utility, not a substitute for Discord's own account security, good role configuration or independent safeguards. For any beta or pre-release version, the service is offered for testing, without any claim of independent security certification. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any warranties, liability limits or commercial terms must be reviewed for the operator's jurisdiction before public use.
