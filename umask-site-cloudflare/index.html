The idea

Simple from the first command

Three steps. No dashboard.

All setup and authentication happen within Discord.

01 — SET UP

Choose your roles

The owner runs /setup and selects a permanent staff role, an elevated role and allowed durations.

02 — ISSUE

Give staff their codes

The owner runs /issue. The staff member collects a unique ten-word phrase with /receive.

03 — USE

Authenticate when needed

Staff run /authenticate and choose a duration. Their elevated role is then removed on sign-out or expiry.*

Least privilege, by design

Small permissions.
One specific job.

umask only manages the temporary role that you choose. It isn't a moderation suite and doesn't inspect everyday conversations.

It must be able to grant the permissions in your chosen elevated role, and its own role must be placed above that role in Discord's hierarchy. It does not need to be at the top of the hierarchy.

Good to know

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Security tools should be clear about what they can—and can't—protect.

Does umask change my server's permissions?

No. The server owner configures the elevated role and its permissions in Discord. umask controls when an authorised staff member receives or loses that role.

Does it protect staff while they're signed in?

Not fully. While a session is active, the moderator has the elevated role. umask primarily reduces exposure while they're signed out. It also assumes the authentication phrase hasn't been stolen.

What if umask goes offline?

Discord roles aren't automatically revoked by Discord when a bot disconnects. On reconnection, umask checks recorded sessions and removes expired roles. During an outage, access may remain active longer than intended.

How are authentication codes stored?

umask stores a one-way password hash. For initial collection, a temporary encrypted copy can be retained for up to 15 minutes. The phrase should be kept safely outside the Discord account it protects.

Can a compromised server owner be protected?

No. The actual server owner can change roles or remove the bot. umask focuses on reducing the risks posed by compromised staff accounts.
